There is one networked PCs in the library for use by current university members. You will need your University account login to sign into these. There are also screens, wireless keyboards and mice for you to connect your own device to in the computing area and at the back of the reading room. The PCs and equipment are on height adjustable desks to allow you to work sitting or standing.

 Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the library by connecting to Eduroam or UniOfCam-IOT.  Details on how to do this are available via the University Information Services website. 

The library has a multifunctional device (MFD) for printing, scanning and photocopying. Further details on how to top up your prining/photocopying credit and how to set up your own device to print to the MFD are available from the University Information Services website.
