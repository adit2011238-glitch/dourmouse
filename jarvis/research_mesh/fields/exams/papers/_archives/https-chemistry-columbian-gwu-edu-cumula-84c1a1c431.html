Cumulative Exam Schedule

Cumulative exam schedule and sign up
 
Elect to take a cumulative exam by selecting the name of the examiner in the area of interest below. Send an email to the examiner notifying them of your desire to take an exam that month. You must sign up by the first day of the month or you may not be able to take the exam in that month. The examiner will arrange for an exam to be given during the selected month.  The examiner will will work with you to schedule the exam. Your results will be returned prior to the start of the next month.  This will allow you to make an informed decision about taking another exam prior to the end of the sign-up period.
