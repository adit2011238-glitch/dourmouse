Computing Affiliates Program

In the College of Computing, we pride ourselves on the rigorous cutting-edge education we provide our students. We teach them to work hard, solve problems creatively, and focus on projects that make a difference. Our goal is to position them in exciting, rewarding careers with employers who value them. 

We look forward to helping you identify and match with students who have the specific skills you need. And we are good at it — last year 60 percent of our students had jobs or internships waiting for them upon graduation (another 37 percent went into academia).

Image

We continue to rise in the rankings, placing #5 for undergraduate education and #6 for graduate education in the most recent U.S. News & World Report data. When you hire our students, they are technically and professionally prepared to make an immediate impact.

We do all of this while educating an ever larger number of students — 6,000 undergraduate and graduate students on-campus and almost 26,000 in our online master’s programs. We anticipate more growth with the announcement of a new Bachelor’s program in Mathematics and Computer Science If you are looking for talent with a wide range of computing-related backgrounds and experiences, we provide an excellent pool. 

Our corporate recruiting manager will help craft a strategy based on your company’s hiring objectives. We are dedicated to helping you hire the talent you need, and we look forward to working with you.  

Vivek Sarkar

Dean and John P. Imlay Jr. Chair of Computing

Partnership Tiers

Buzz Tier
Ideal for startups and companies new to recruiting at the College of Computing, this level focuses on building your brand on campus. You'll gain visibility through signature events like the Fall Career Fair, laying the groundwork for future recruitment success.

Ramblin’ Wreck Level
This tier expands your presence through participation in career preparation workshops, panel discussions, and branding opportunities. It’s designed to help you build deeper connections with students and engage directly with the next generation of computing talent.

Tech Tower Level
Our highest level of engagement, this tier provides access to over 20 student organizations, participation in our annual Hackathon, and opportunities to co-develop specialized programming such as innovation challenges or industry-specific workshops. It’s ideal for companies looking to build lasting relationships and position themselves as leaders in the field.
